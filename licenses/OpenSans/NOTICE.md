# Open Sans font attribution

Included files: `OpenSans-Regular.ttf` and `OpenSans-Semibold.ttf` under `src/WebExpress.WebIndex.WiUI/Resources/Fonts/` report version 1.10 in their embedded name records. Both declare the Apache License, Version 2.0. The accompanying [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt) contains that license.

Regular attribution: the embedded copyright record identifies digitized data copyright 2010 to 2011, Google Corporation.

Semibold attribution: the embedded copyright record identifies digitized data copyright 2011, Google Corporation.

License evidence: these statements come from name records 0, 5, 13 and 14 of the bundled files. They do not rely on the licensing metadata of a newer Open Sans release. SHA-256 hashes are recorded in the WiUI SBOM.
